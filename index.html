// The Man Behind the Wheel

Built From Nothing.
Built to Last.

Alexander Ghebrehiwot
Founder & CEO — EXPO Trucking LLC

Alexander Ghebrehiwot didn't inherit a trucking empire — he built one. Born in Eritrea and driven by a vision that hard work and persistence could build something real on American soil, Alexander arrived with more determination than resources — and turned that into one of Dallas's rising independent carriers.

In 2017, he founded EXPO Trucking LLC from the ground up in Dallas, Texas. What started as a single-truck operation has grown into a 12-unit interstate fleet with 21 professional CDL drivers, running freight across state lines with a zero-crash safety record — a standard most fleets can only aspire to.

Today, Alex isn't just running a trucking company — he's building a technology-powered logistics operation. With AI-driven dispatch systems, real-time fleet intelligence, and a partnership with Jafear AI, EXPO Trucking is positioning itself at the intersection of blue-collar hustle and Silicon Valley innovation. The next chapter isn't just more trucks — it's smarter ones.
